Privacy policy

Video Compressor · last updated 3 November 2026

Your videos stay on your device

Video Compressor processes your files inside the app on your own computer or phone. Videos, audio, transcripts and notes are never uploaded, sent to us, or shared with anyone. Files you save go only where you choose to save them.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not use cookies.

Downloads the app makes

  • The app itself is loaded from GitHub Pages (github.com), which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses.
  • When you use Transcribe & notes for the first time, the app downloads the Whisper speech model from Hugging Face (huggingface.co) and its software from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). Only the model files are requested; your audio is not sent. These services may keep standard server logs.

The app stores a copy of itself and of the speech model in your browser's storage so it can start faster and work offline. You can remove this by uninstalling the app or clearing its site data.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. For questions, open an issue at github.com/rhhas-knust/Video-Compressor/issues.
